# Privacy Policy — Al Quran: Hifz, Audio, Tafsir

**Effective Date:** 2026-11-16  
**Package Name:** `com.salmanul.alquran`

**Al Quran: Hifz, Audio, Tafsir** is built with a strict **privacy-first, offline-first** philosophy. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track your personal reading habits.

## 1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
Your bookmarks, personal notes, Hifz memorization progress, study streaks, Tasbih counts, and app settings are stored **locally on your device**. You can export or restore them anytime using the built-in Backup & Restore feature.

## 2. Permissions Used (and Why)
- **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`) — Optional:** Used only when you actively start the **Voice Verse Finder** or **Tasmee' AI Recitation Exam**. Audio is processed for recitation matching and is not sold or used for advertising.
- **Location (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`) — Optional:** Used only to calculate accurate **5-Waqt Prayer Times** and **Qibla Compass** direction for your area. You can also choose a city manually.
- **Notifications & Exact Alarms — Optional:** Used only for **Adhan alerts**, **Friday/Surah Al-Mulk reminders**, and **Hifz study reminders** that you enable.
- **Internet Access — Optional:** Used only when you choose to download Tafsirs, translations, reciter audio files, or fetch online prayer timetable adjustments. Core reading, Hifz, morphology, and offline prayer calculations work without internet.

## 3. Zero Ads & Zero Data Selling
- **No third-party ad networks.**
- **No selling or renting of user data.**
