## Why Al Quran?

Most Quran and Hifz apps either lock AI voice memorization behind expensive yearly subscriptions (`$50–$60/year`) or interrupt worship with banner and video ads.

**Al Quran: Hifz, Audio, Tafsir** gives every Muslim full access to advanced Hifz, Tafsir, Grammar, Audio, and Prayer tools **completely free, with zero ads and zero subscriptions.**

## Key Features

### 1. Hifz & Tasmee' AI Recitation Exam
- **Voice-Powered Tasmee' Exam:** Recite any Surah or custom Ayah range and get real-time word-by-word accuracy grading.
- **Smart Hifz Presets & Next-Range Chaining:** Practice in bite-sized blocks and jump straight to the next range in one tap.
- **Spaced Repetition (SRS) & Progress Tracking:** Track memorized Ayahs, 30-Juz mastery, 90-day study heatmaps, and 30 unlockable achievements.

### 2. Voice Verse Finder ("Shazam for Quran")
- Forget which Surah an Ayah is in? Recite a few words and match across all **6,236 verses** instantly offline.

### 3. Mushaf Reader (604 Pages)
- **True Sub-Pixel Justified Mushaf Layout:** Clean Uthmanic and Indo-Pak Tajweed rendering across 604 pages.
- **8 Reading Themes & Auto-Scroll:** Comfortable day, sepia, and dark themes with 10-step auto-scroll speed and Ayah highlighting.
- **Interactive Tajweed Guide:** Visual diagrams and rules for Makharij, Noon/Meem Sakinah, Qalqalah, Madd, and Waqf.

### 4. Deep Study Mode (Tadabbur & Grammar)
- **40 Tafsirs in 15 Languages** + **16 Word-by-Word (WBW) Editions** with WBW audio playback.
- **Offline Quranic Arabic Corpus:** Tap any word to inspect its **Triliteral Root, Lemma, Sarf (morphology), and I'rab (grammar)** without an internet connection.
- **Surah Overviews, Quranic Duas & Topics Index.**

### 5. Audio & Quranify Player
- **12 Curated Reciters** with verse-by-verse sync, looping, background playback, and offline downloads.

### 6. Daily Worship & Home Widgets
- **5-Waqt Adhan & Astronomical Engine:** Offline prayer time calculation with customizable Adhan/Takbir notifications and Suhoor/Iftar/Friday reminders.
- **Qibla Compass, Digital Tasbih & Hijri Calendar.**
- **5 Home Screen Widgets:** Prayer Times, Ayah of the Day, Last Read, Khatm Planner, and Tasbih.
- **8 UI Languages:** English, Arabic, Urdu, Indonesian, Turkish, French, Hindi, and Malayalam.
- **10-Category Local Backup & Restore:** Keep your bookmarks, notes, Hifz progress, and settings safe.
